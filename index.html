Key Features

  • Summary & Active Energy: Live steps, distance, active minutes, stand hours, and interactive D / W / M / 6M / Y active energy charts with BMR and food logging.
  • Vitals & Body Measurements: 24/7 heart rate, resting HR, SpO2, sleep stages (Deep, Core, REM), stress tracking, and Weight & BMI with healthy weight guidance.
  • Workouts & GNSS Routes: 7-day Duration, Calories, and Distance trends plus per-workout pace, cadence, stride length, heart rate zones, and GPS route map.
  • 900+ FMC Watch Faces: Infinite scroll, Jump-to-Page slider, search & sort filters, Saved bookmarks, 2x/3x grid density, and direct .bin download to Download/Watch Face.
  • Watch Widgets Editor: Read, customize, reorder, and restore default Quick Card widget screens on the watch.
  • Device Tools & Firmware: App notification bridge, quick replies, alarms, contacts, weather auto-refresh, firmware version readout, and local AOTA flashing.

How to Install

  1. Click Download on the right (or open the Releases tab).
  2. Download the APK for your phone (arm64-v8a for most modern Android devices).
  3. Open the .apk file and allow Install from unknown sources if prompted.
  4. Enable Bluetooth & Location, then pair your CMF Watch Pro 2 inside the app.
