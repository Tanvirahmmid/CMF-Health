Key Features

Summary & Active Energy

Live steps, distance, active minutes, stand hours, and interactive D / W / M / 6M / Y active energy charts with BMR and food logging.

Heart, SpO2, Sleep & BMI

24/7 heart rate monitoring, resting HR, SpO2, sleep stage breakdown (Deep, Core, REM), stress tracking, and BMI with healthy weight guidance.

Workouts & GNSS Routes

7-day Duration, Calories, and Distance trends plus per-workout pace, cadence, stride length, heart rate zones, and GPS route map.

900+ FMC Watch Faces

Infinite scroll, Jump-to-Page slider, search & sort filters, Saved bookmarks, 2x/3x grid density, and direct .bin download to phone storage.

Watch Widgets Editor

Read, customize, reorder, and restore default Quick Card widget screens directly on your CMF Watch Pro 2.

Notifications & Firmware

App notification forwarding, quick replies, alarms, contacts, weather auto-refresh, firmware version readout, and local AOTA flashing.

How to Install

  1. 1
    Tap Download Latest APK above to open the latest release on GitHub.
  2. 2
    Under Assets, download app-arm64-v8a-release.apk (recommended for most modern Android phones).
  3. 3
    Open the downloaded .apk file and allow Install from unknown sources if prompted.
  4. 4
    Enable Bluetooth & Location, then pair your CMF Watch Pro 2 inside the app.
