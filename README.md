# CMF Health

Companion Android app for **CMF Watch Pro 2** — featuring live health & fitness tracking, workout & GNSS route history, heart rate, SpO₂, sleep analysis, nutrition & calorie tracking, and custom watch face uploads.

## Download APK

Download the latest Android APK from the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** page:

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** on the right (or top) of this repository.
2. Under **Assets**, tap **`CMF-Health.apk`** (or `app-release.apk`) to download.
3. Open the downloaded `.apk` on your Android phone and allow **Install from unknown sources** if prompted.

## Supported Device

- **CMF Watch Pro 2** (BLE)
